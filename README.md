# **BMW Sales Analysis Report (2019–2023)**



---

## **Objective**

To perform a comprehensive analysis of BMW global vehicle sales from 2019 to 2023, uncover performance trends, identify top-performing models and regions, and derive actionable insights using Power BI.

---

## **Tools & Techniques**

* **Tool Used**: Power BI  
* **Processes**:
  * Built a robust **data model** linking sales, car models, regions, and channels  
  * Created clean and insightful **DAX measures** for quantity variance, revenue growth, and KPI tracking  
  * Utilized **UNICHAR and SVG** visuals for intuitive signals (arrows, flags, sparklines)  
  * Applied **conditional formatting, slicers, and filters** to allow smooth exploration  
  * Designed visuals with **storytelling flow**, enabling users to navigate from macro to micro insights

## **Key Insights**

### 🚗 **Sales Performance Overview**

* **Total Revenue (2019–2023)**: **$1.13 billion**  
* **Total Quantity Sold**: **15,002 units**  
* **YoY Growth (2023 vs 2022)**:
  * Revenue: **-4.3%** (from $230M to $220M)  
  * Quantity Sold: **-5.3%** (from 3,078 to 2,916)

---

### 🔝 **Top Selling Models**

| Model        | Units Sold |
| ------------ | ---------- |
| **BMW Z4**   | 666        |
| BMW 8 Series | 641        |
| BMW M4       | 620        |
| BMW i8       | 615        |

* **Most Expensive**: BMW M8 ($79.1K), BMW iX3 ($78.2K)  
* **Average Price Across Models**: **$75.2K**

---

### 📊 **BMW Z4 Country-Specific Trends (2022–2023)**

| Country      | 2022 Z4 Units | 2023 Z4 Units | Change   |
| ------------ | ------------- | ------------- | -------- |
| South Africa | 10            | 14            | +40%     |
| Spain        | 15            | 2             | **-87%** |
| Mexico       | 16            | 12            | -25%     |
| US           | 12            | 11            | -8%      |
| Canada       | 12            | 8             | -33%     |
| Argentina    | 20            | 7             | **-65%** |

* While most countries saw a **decline in Z4 sales**, **South Africa bucked the trend** with notable growth.  
* The steep drops in **Spain and Argentina** highlight the need for **local strategy reevaluation**.

---

### 🌍 **Market Distribution by Region**

#### 🔝 High Revenue Countries:

* **North America**: US, Mexico, Canada  
* **Africa**: Nigeria, Kenya, Egypt  
* **Asia**: China, South Korea, Japan  
* **South America**: Argentina, Chile, Colombia  
* **Europe**: Spain

#### ⚠️ Low Revenue Countries:

* **Europe**: Italy, France, Germany, UK  
* **Asia**: India, Thailand  
* **Africa**: Morocco, South Africa (despite Z4 rise)  
* **South America**: Brazil, Peru

---

### 🛒 **Sales Channel Breakdown (2023)**

* **Wholesale**: 7K (45%)  
* **Dealerships**: 5K (33%)  
* **Online**: 3K (22%)

> The wholesale channel continues to lead, but **online sales are growing**, indicating a shift in buyer behavior.

---

## **Conclusions & Recommendations**

### 1. **Model-Specific Strategy**

* Investigate why **BMW Z4** sales are falling in key regions (Spain, Argentina).  
* Consider **local consumer preferences**, **economic conditions**, and **marketing effectiveness**.  
* Double down on **regions like South Africa** showing increasing interest.

### 2. **Regional Optimization**

* **Re-engage underperforming markets** in Europe and Asia through targeted promotions or model relaunches.  
* Leverage success in **Nigeria and Latin America** with tailored offerings and regional partnerships.

### 3. **Digital Expansion**

* Strengthen **online sales strategy**, particularly in tech-savvy or underserved markets.

### 4. **Performance Tracking**

* Continue refining dashboards with **interactive filters**, **trendlines**, and **predictive analytics** to anticipate future demand.
